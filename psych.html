Core Strategy for Analytical Minds

How to Avoid the "Robot Trap" With Dr. Jake Tremblay

Because you are naturally disciplined, analytical, and objective (tracking macros/workouts, writing 1,500-word tech logs, compartmentalizing stress), there is one specific trap in psychological testing: sounding like an emotionless machine or claiming impossible perfection.

The Golden Formula:
✅ 85% Grounded Discipline & Routine
✅ 15% Human Warmth, Empathy & Humility
❌ 0% "I have zero emotions / I never make mistakes"
🚩 Red Flag #1: The "Saint" Spike (L-r Scale)

In the MMPI-3, there are ~14 questions called Uncommon Virtues (L-r). They ask things like: "I never get annoyed," "I have never told a lie," "I like everyone I meet." If you click FALSE to normal human flaws because you want to look disciplined, the computer flags your test as Invalid / Defensive Faking Good.

🚩 Red Flag #2: Emotional Numbness

When discussing graphic Body Worn Video with Dr. Tremblay, saying "I have zero emotions, it's just pixels on a screen to me" sounds like dissociation or lack of human empathy. Instead, say: "I naturally feel human empathy when I see someone hurt, and I channel that into doing a meticulous technical job so justice is served."

🚩 Red Flag #3: The "Lone Wolf" Trap (MPQ)

On the MPQ, high self-reliance is great, but if you answer every social question with "I prefer to be completely alone and never rely on others," it flags low Social Closeness. Balance your independent focus with your 5 close NAIT DMIT friends, Discord collab projects, and team handoffs.

10-Point "Robotic vs. Grounded Human" Calibration Matrix

Read these side-by-side comparisons to tune your instinct before November 20th.

1. Viewing Graphic Trauma Footage
❌ Robotic / Stoic Trap: "I have zero emotional bias. When I watch graphic violence, I feel nothing at all. I just look at waveforms and pixels like a machine."
✅ Grounded, Healthy Human: "I'm human—if I see a child or victim in distress, I recognize it's tragic and feel empathy. What helps me succeed is shifting into a purposeful, task-focused mindset at work so their evidence is protected, and then using my running and gym routine after shift to decompress."
2. Everyday Irritability & Patience
❌ Robotic / Saint Trap: "I never get annoyed, frustrated, or impatient with anyone under any circumstances." (Spikes L-r Lie Scale)
✅ Grounded, Healthy Human: "Like anyone, I can feel a brief flash of frustration if someone is being unreasonable or traffic is backed up, but I don't take it personally or snap at people. I take a breath, listen actively, and focus on solving the problem."
3. Pre-Event Nerves & Sleep
❌ Robotic / Stoic Trap: "I never feel nervous before big tests or interviews, and I never have a night of light sleep."
✅ Grounded, Healthy Human: "Most nights I sleep great. Before a huge milestone that I really care about—like my EPS panel interview—it's normal for me to have some excited anticipation and sleep a bit lighter, which I channel into sharp preparation."
4. Leaving TELUS (Employment History)
❌ Defensive / Bitter Trap: "Management was unfair and toxic, or nothing ever went wrong."
✅ Grounded, Healthy Human: "I'm really proud of my years in the field at TELUS—12,000+ incident-free hours and solving complex customer issues. Over time, the role evolved heavily toward aggressive doorstep sales quotas rather than technical quality. That clashed with my strengths as a thorough technician, so parting ways allowed me to pursue a structured public-service role where quality and accuracy come first."
5. White Lies & Minor Faults
❌ Robotic / Saint Trap: Answering TRUE to: "I have never told a lie" or FALSE to: "I sometimes put off until tomorrow what I ought to do today."
✅ Grounded, Healthy Human: Admitting everyday human realities: yes, I've told a polite white lie before; yes, on a lazy Sunday I might put off a household chore until Monday. In serious work and ethical matters, my integrity is 100% solid.
6. Social Connection vs. Independence
❌ Isolated Robot Trap: "I just stick to myself, track my personal metrics, and don't need social connection."
✅ Grounded, Healthy Human: "I'm comfortable working independently at a workstation for hours, AND I really value my close circle—five of my closest friends are still from my NAIT DMIT cohort, we collaborate on creative game-dev projects on Discord every week, and I love building friendly rapport with coworkers."
